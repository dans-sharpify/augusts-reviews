★★★★★Rating: 5 out of 517 Aug 2018

This place got recommended to me from a local. It is a hidden gem of a hair salon. I got a warm welcome and a coffee to start my visit. The owner of the salon took her time to figure out what to do with my hair and talked to me about what to do. I had some dark "highlights" in my blond hair which was hard to fix. Fixing someone else's work is not easy and I have been very unhappy with my hair for a long time. She took the time to find them and dye them lighter to achieve my wished hair style. I am really happy with the end result and the prize is absolutely fair for the time and effort. She also shared a lot of local tips with me during the appointment. They have a lot of crazy hair styles on their Facebook side, but don't get misleaded by that, they also do traditional styles very well. It is a place for young people with good taste. High quality work. Make sure to book an appointment via Facebook. They do not need advertising since they have people coming back, which is the best review this place can get. Will go again if I am back in Riga.
